Risograph printing celebrates texture, vibrant colour, and the beauty of small imperfections. Every layer carries its own personality.

Inspired by independent magazines, art books, and experimental posters, this design explores the playful nature of analogue printing. Slight ink misalignment, visible grain, and bold colour combinations create compositions that feel tactile, expressive, and unmistakably human. Rather than hiding imperfections, Risograph transforms them into part of the visual language.
